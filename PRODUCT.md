# Product

## Register
brand

## Users
- **Visitors:** People checking the organization's profile, portfolio, and activities.
- **Administrators:** Staff who edit the website, update information, upload photos, post blogs, and manage events.

## Product Purpose
An editable organization profile website that showcases the organization's work and activities while allowing admins to easily update content.

## Brand Personality
Modern, Lime Green & Black (striking, tech-forward, high-contrast).

## Anti-references
- Cluttered, busy interfaces that are hard to use.
- The standard "AI-generated" look (e.g., generic cream/sand backgrounds, nested cards, aphoristic copy).

## Design Principles
- **Clear Information Architecture:** Keep it organized and not visually cluttered.
- **Striking Contrast:** Use the lime green and black theme effectively for a modern feel.
- **Authentic Craft:** Avoid AI-slop tropes; ensure genuine, intentional design choices.

## Accessibility & Inclusion
Ensure proper contrast between the lime green accent and black backgrounds for readability.
